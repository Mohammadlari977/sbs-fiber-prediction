Methodology & Disclaimer
Eight machine-learning models trained on 344 literature data points from 47 SBS papers. Inputs: polymer concentration, intrinsic viscosity, c/c*, solvent surface tension, solvent boiling point, air pressure, feed rate, and working distance. Target: ln(fiber diameter). Intrinsic viscosity is computed from Mark–Houwink constants, c* = 1/([η]·ρsolvent), and the surface tension, boiling point, and density of mixtures are weighted by volume fraction. Zero-shot mode uses the pre-trained models directly. Calibration mode applies the frozen models to your conditions and fits an affine correction (ln dcorrected = a · ln dpredicted + b) to your measured diameters; the models are not retrained and your data are not combined with the literature data. A warning is shown when inputs lie outside the domain of the training data. The models predict diameter for conditions that form fibers and do not predict whether fibers will form. Predictions are estimates for pre-screening and ranking of conditions; confirm diameters experimentally.
